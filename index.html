Operating conditions & source notes

The listed motors use 415 V ±10%, 50 Hz ±5%, Class F insulation, 50°C ambient and altitude below 1000 m above mean sea level. The catalogue states IEC 60034-1 and IS 12615:2018.

HP and kW are the catalogue’s paired ratings. Results use exact listed HP values, without interpolation or automatic rounding up. Multiple entries are shown together. Catalogue prices are INR list prices from this edition.

ABB document links are taken from the supplied PDF. Where a link is absent from the PDF, it is omitted here. This is an independent selection aid.
